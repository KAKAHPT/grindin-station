# M13 — Rankings (points, Elo, teams) and event filters

**Date:** 2026-11-05. **Status:** approved design (mock `public/mock/rankings.html`: three tabs, shared filters, two "how it works" pop-ups). **Scope:** the public `/rankings` page replacing the circuits list as the "Rankings" destination, a per-event country, and the filter bar on `/events`. Circuits keep their pages and points untouched.

## 1. Problem

"Rankings" today lists organizer circuits, each with its own points. The organiser wants one place with every player and every tournament: a points ranking, an Elo leaderboard and a team ranking, all narrowed by the same filters (season, country, organizer, format, team, search), and the same filters on the events list so players find tournaments in their country or from an organizer.

## 2. Decisions (with the organiser, on the mock)

| # | Decision |
|---|----------|
| D1 | Three tabs under the Rankings title: **Ranking points** ("points added over events"), **Leaderboard** ("Elo rating"), **Teams** ("team ranking"). The filter bar sits under the tabs and applies to all three. |
| D2 | Filters: search (player name, handle or team; on Teams the team name), **Season** segmented control (current year, previous years with data, "All time"), **Country** (players' country; on `/events` the event's country), **Organizer**, **Format** (cEDH · pods of four / 1v1), **Team**. Active filters show as chips with ✕ and "Clear all". Filters live in the URL (`?season=2026&country=PT&organizer=cedh-pt&format=multiplayer&team=…&q=…`) so a filtered ranking can be shared. |
| D3 | **Season = calendar year** of the event's start date. The current year is selected by default. |
| D4 | **Ranking points per event (option B, field-weighted):** per game **in Swiss rounds and league pods only** (cut rounds give the bonuses, in every format), win +3, draw +1, loss 0, bye +1 (a bye is an `Advance` in a Swiss round; a 1v1 match win counts as one win whatever the games). Bonuses per tournament: made the top cut +5, reached the top 4 +5 more, won the tournament +10 more. The event total is multiplied by a **field factor** from the number of non-waitlisted participants: ≤ 16 → ×1, 17–32 → ×1.25, 33–64 → ×1.5, > 64 → ×2. Losses never subtract. Leagues: games count the same; no cut bonuses; factor from the league's participants. Penalties count as losses (they are `Loss` subclasses today). |
| D5 | **Leaderboard = Elo**, start 1500, K = 32, expected score `1 / (1 + 10^((Rb − Ra)/400))`, update `R' = R + K (S − E)`. A multiplayer pod is scored as a duel between every pair of its seated players: the winner scores 1 against each other player, the others 0 against the winner and 0.5 against each other; a drawn pod is all draws; an `Advance`/bye is not a game; a player without a current result in a finished pod (dropped, no-show) is skipped for that pod. 1v1 matches are one duel (Win/Draw/Loss by the match result). Pods are processed in order of event start time, then round number, then pod number. Players with fewer than 10 rated games are **provisional** (shown with a mark, listed after established players by default). |
| D6 | Elo is computed over the whole season ("All time": all finished events ever). Country, organizer, format and team filters only narrow **who is listed and which events are counted for ranking points**; they never change a rating (a filtered leaderboard shows the season rating of the matching players, and the organizer/format filters list the players who played at least one matching event). |
| D7 | **Teams:** a player's contribution in the filtered events = ranking points + 15 × top cuts + 30 × tournament wins; the team score = the sum of its **five best** contributions. The team is the one on the player's account at the time the standings are computed (snapshot per contribution row). Teams with one player are listed. Columns: team, team points, average rating of the counted players, players, events, wins, top cuts. |
| D8 | Each tab has a note under the table and a "How is it calculated?" link opening a **modal** (same frame as the app's modal layout) with two parts: the plain-words version ("the short version", marbles) and the detailed version with the formulas, a worked example and the house rules. The Ranking points modal explains D4 with a table and an example; the Leaderboard modal D5 (mock text); the Teams modal D7 (mock text). All three in en/pt-PT/es. |
| D9 | Summary strip above the table, grey: players, events, organizers, last update (the time the standings were last rebuilt). |
| D10 | Table columns. Points: #, player (avatar initials/photo, name, team tag, handle, country), points, events, wins, top cuts, conversion, best finish; sortable by the numeric columns. Leaderboard: #, player, rating, change (since the previous rebuild), peak, games, win %. Teams as D7. 20 rows then "Show all N". The top three ranks in `text-ink`, the rest muted. Player names link to `/p/:handle` (only for visible profiles; hidden profiles show the name without a link and no handle). |
| D11 | **Storage:** a `ranking_contributions` table, one row per (player, event), rebuilt by `Rankings::RebuildJob` (nightly at 05:00 and after any event finishes or a result is corrected, debounced) holding: `player_id`, `event_id`, `season` (year), `country` (player's at rebuild), `team` (player's at rebuild), `event_organizer_id`, `format`, `field` (participants), `wins`, `draws`, `losses`, `byes`, `made_cut`, `top4`, `won`, `points` (decimal 8,2). And an `elo_ratings` table, one row per (player, season or `nil` = all time): `rating`, `peak`, `games`, `wins`, `previous_rating`. Both are read-only for the web layer; pages never compute points on the fly. |
| D12 | `events.country` (2 letters, nullable) edited in the event Settings next to the address, defaulting to the organizer user's country when the event is created; shown nowhere else yet. `/events` filters by it; events without a country only appear with "All countries". |
| D13 | `/events` gets the same bar: search (event name, organizer, city), season (year of start), country, organizer, format, chips, all as URL params over the existing paginated scopes. The "Upcoming / Past" switch stays. |
| D14 | The topbar "Rankings" link goes to `/rankings`. The circuits list moves to `/circuits` (unchanged page) and is linked from the Rankings page footer note ("Organizer circuits keep their own standings: see circuits"). |
| D16 | **Event tier (admin only):** `events.tier` ∈ `none` (default) \| `bronze` \| `silver` \| `gold` \| `platinum`, set on the admin Events page next to Featured (a select per row, `PATCH /admin/events/:id/tier`, audited `tier_changed`). Tiers feed an **invitational leaderboard** visible only at `/admin/invitational`: per player and season, invitational points = ranking points of the event × tier multiplier (bronze ×1, silver ×1.5, gold ×2, platinum ×3; `none` → 0), stored as `invitational_points` on the contribution row. The admin page has the season filter, the same table shape as Ranking points plus a "tier events" column, and an "export CSV" link (same CSV rules as M10). Nothing about tiers shows on public pages. |
| D17 | **Admin "Organizers" tab:** the waiting-list signups (`waitlist_signups` gains `approved_at`, `approved_by_id`, `declined_at`) with name, e-mail, date, whether an account with that e-mail exists, and actions: **Approve** (requires an account: creates the `EventOrganizer` via `User#initialize_organizer` unless one exists, stamps `approved_at`/`approved_by_id`, audits `organizer_approved`, sends `OrganizersMailer#approved` "you can now organise" with a link to `/organizer`), **Decline** (stamps `declined_at`, audits `organizer_declined`, no e-mail). A signup without an account shows "No account yet" and only Decline; the row turns approvable once the person signs up with that e-mail. Below, the current organizers (name, e-mail, events count, since) with **Revoke** (destroys the `EventOrganizer` only when it has no events, audits `organizer_revoked`). Filters: Pending (default) / Approved / Declined. |
| D15 | Grey only (M12 rules); the only accent is the positive rating change (`+12` in accent, negative in danger). Mobile: the summary strip in two columns, the secondary columns hidden under 640 px as in the mock. |

## 3. Out of scope

Points for events before this ships are computed by the first rebuild (historic data included). Seasons by anything other than calendar year, team pages, Elo decay, per-organizer point tables, changing circuit points.

## 4. Acceptance

1. `/rankings` shows the three tabs, the filter bar, chips and the summary strip; with the mock's shapes (players with contributions over two years, three countries, two organizers, two formats, three teams) each filter narrows the three tables as D2–D7 say, and the URL carries the filters.
2. `Rankings::Points.for(participant)` gives the D4 numbers for: a 16-player winner with 3 wins and a draw (30), the same at 80 players (60), a top-16 player eliminated in the first cut round with 2-1-1 (12 × factor), a league player with 4 wins (12 × factor), a bye (+1).
3. `Rankings::Elo` reproduces the worked example (Ana 1500 beating 1600/1500/1400 → 1548 after the pod, within rounding) and marks players under 10 games provisional.
4. Team points = sum of the five best contributions with the 15/30 bonuses (unit test with six players where the sixth is dropped).
5. The three modals open from their notes, contain the short and detailed parts, close with ✕, Escape and outside click (system test).
6. `/events?country=PT&organizer=<handle>&season=2026&format=one_v_one&q=porto` lists only matching events; chips show the four filters; "Clear all" returns to `/events`.
7. The rebuild job is idempotent (running twice gives identical rows), runs nightly and after `FinishTournamentJob`/`FinishLeagueJob` and after a result correction (`result_corrected` audit) with a 5-minute debounce.
8. Prosopite raises nothing on `/rankings` and `/events` (fixed query counts).
10. On `/admin/organizers` a pending signup whose e-mail has an account is approved: the user gains an organizer, the row moves to Approved, the audit log has `organizer_approved` and an e-mail is delivered; a signup without an account cannot be approved; Decline moves it to Declined; Revoke on an organizer with events is refused.
9. An admin sets a tournament to gold on `/admin/events`; after the rebuild `/admin/invitational` lists its players with ranking points × 2, the audit log has `tier_changed`, and a non-admin gets 404 on `/admin/invitational`; `/rankings` and the event pages show no tier.
